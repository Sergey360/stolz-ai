# Coverage and claim record

Use an existing suitable task record. For a substantial multi-source analysis,
derivative deliverable or handoff, keep this compact Markdown index beside the
work. Do not store secrets, large source copies or invented IDs. A record is
an index of evidence, not independent proof of reading or understanding.

## Requested basis

- Requested sources and exact reading/analysis scope.
- Intended decisions, deliverables and source-dependent acceptance conditions.

## Coverage

| Actual source / available identity | Source kind | Access | Inspected scope / evidence locator | Supports | Gap and affected requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Separate access from reading. Access may be retrieved, blocked, not attempted
or not found within a stated search boundary. Reading may be none, partial
with named sections/ranges, or throughout the requested scope. A downloaded
PDF may be unread; a retrieved page may contain only metadata. If coverage of
earlier reading is unavailable, record it as unknown, not known non-reading.

Source kinds matter: metadata, description, syllabus, lesson, specification,
tool observation and another agent's report support different claims. Record
the latter as a separate attributed source. Observe pagination, extraction
limits and truncation. Name the received portion; retrieve missing relevant
portions when possible, without upgrading the original partial observation.

Use actual URLs/paths and observation dates for mutable sources. Use available
revisions or hashes when identity matters. Do not manufacture hashes for bytes
you do not possess. A negative search records its queries and directories,
account or time window; it does not establish universal absence.

## Claims and application

| Claim / decision / output | Kind | Inspected supporting content | Reasoning / concrete application | Open dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Kinds: observed, attributed, inferred, proposed, unknown. Show source section
-> supported finding -> concrete use. Explain inference when it affects the
decision. A bibliography alone does not establish this chain. Preserve
contradictions and uncertainties instead of averaging them into certainty.

## Existing STOLZ records

When an existing context fragment is used, reference its actual `fragment_id`,
`delta_identity`, `content_sha256` and recorded range. These belong to the
existing context ledger; do not copy raw fragments or generate a replacement
ledger. Check identity, invalidation and freshness through the existing
context/reuse route before substitution. An index may also reference a verified
result's existing key and evidence locator. Missing references stay missing.

Add semantic coverage and applications here, not to the stable local-state API.
A compact ledger projection is not the source text: fetch the relevant bounded
content when the decision requires it. A reused result retains its producer,
observed scope, evidence and gaps; it does not become a new personal reading.

## Completion and correction

- Requirements actually completed, their evidence and observed environment.
- Remaining requirements, material gaps and provisional outputs.
- Corrections to prior statements and affected decisions/outputs.
- Next verification or continuation needed, without expanding authorization.

On handoff send this index and exact artifact/version pointers. Dispatch,
receipt, implementation, local verification, target-system acceptance and
publication remain distinct. The receiver verifies inherited material claims
as needed and attributes unchecked reports. No elapsed wait or "done" message
establishes acceptance. Preserve gaps across handoffs and compaction.
