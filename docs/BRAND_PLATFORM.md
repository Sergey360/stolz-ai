# STOLZ A.I. Brand Platform

This document is the approved public-content and visual-direction source supplied by the project operator. It governs naming, positioning, tone, compatibility language, and future visual assets. It is not a trademark-clearance opinion.

## Name

- Product name: **STOLZ A.I.**
- Technical identifier for repositories, packages, directories, and URLs: `stolz-ai`
- Optional parent-brand form: **STOLZ A.I. by IT360**
- Full descriptor: **STOLZ A.I. — Token-efficient skills for Codex**

The product name is always written in Latin characters. Keep the periods in `A.I.` in logos and marketing copy because they carry the name's double meaning. Use `stolz-ai` only where a technical identifier is required.

## Name Origin

The name refers to Andrei Ivanovich Stolz, the rational, active, and practical character in Ivan Goncharov's novel *Oblomov*.

`A.I.` has two meanings:

- **Andrei Ivanovich**;
- **Artificial Intelligence**.

The order `STOLZ A.I.` recalls the Russian bibliographic form “Stolz A. I.” while remaining legible internationally as an AI-product name.

## Product Essence

STOLZ A.I. is a skill suite for Codex and compatible AI coding agents that reduces wasted tokens without reducing quality, accuracy, verification, or reliability. It helps an agent:

- load only necessary context;
- avoid rereading unchanged files and instructions;
- prevent repeated reasoning, commands, and tool calls;
- choose the smallest sufficient route;
- apply progressive disclosure and just-in-time context;
- reuse verified results;
- route work more precisely between skills, tools, and stages;
- preserve verification quality with fewer wasteful operations.

Core principle:

> **STOLZ A.I. does not make the model think less. It helps it waste less.**

Efficiency comes from architecture, context discipline, and workflow design—not from shorter answers or skipped checks.

## Positioning

English short description:

> **A token-efficient skill suite for Codex.**

Russian short description:

> **Рациональный набор skills для Codex, сокращающий расход токенов без потери качества работы агента.**

Extended positioning:

> **STOLZ A.I. turns token use from an incidental cost of agent work into a managed engineering resource.**

The audience is developers and teams that use coding agents intensively, work with expensive models or constrained plans, or operate on large codebases and want the same or better result with less context and compute waste.

## Brand Idea

Stolz is not a miser and the brand does not promote saving at any cost. He represents a rational operator who understands resource value, plans before acting, prefers systems to improvisation, avoids expenditure that creates no value, and reaches the result without needless fuss.

For STOLZ A.I., tokens are a managed resource alongside time, memory, compute, and money.

## Character and Voice

The brand is rational, disciplined, technically literate, calm, confident, intelligent, mildly ironic, practical rather than ascetic, and economical rather than cheap.

Communication must be precise, compact, and evidence-led. Avoid hype, pseudo-science, magical-button framing, and unsupported promises. Prefer measurable engineering evidence: excluded context, prevented repeated operations, token delta, tool-call or model-wakeup delta, and confirmation that outcome quality remained equivalent.

## Visual Direction

Combine classical book culture with contemporary engineering minimalism:

- rigorous typography;
- a dominant `STOLZ` with a smaller but unmistakable `A.I.`;
- an editorial serif paired with a technical monospaced face;
- motifs of order, structure, accounting, and optimization;
- a restrained palette;
- minimal decoration.

Do not use caricatures of a miser, coins, piggy banks, money bags, literal character portraits, actors from screen adaptations, modern book illustrations or covers, OpenAI's logo, or a visual identity that could be confused with OpenAI.

## Compatibility and Independence

`Codex` describes compatibility and is not part of the product mark. Public copy must not imply that OpenAI developed, approved, sponsors, or officially supports STOLZ A.I.

Use this notice where compatibility is discussed publicly:

> **STOLZ A.I. is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. Codex is a trademark of OpenAI.**

Provider adapters must use the same rule: describe compatibility accurately without implying affiliation or endorsement.

## Literary and Trademark Boundaries

The original novel *Oblomov* and Goncharov's literary character are in the public domain. Do not reuse modern illustrations, covers, adaptation imagery, actor likenesses, modern edition design, or third-party logos without separate permission.

Availability of `STOLZ A.I.` as a trademark must be checked separately for each intended jurisdiction and relevant software or IT-service class before registration or a claim of exclusivity.
